# Haelo Design System

## Brand Personality

Haelo should feel:

- Calm
- Reflective
- Creative
- Warm
- Youthful, but not childish
- Modern
- Personal
- Safe
- Encouraging

The interface should focus on self-discovery and growth rather than judgment, competition, or performance.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary — Deep Violet

Hex: `#5B4B8A`

Use for:
- Logo
- Navigation
- Primary buttons
- Important headings
- VoiceMap visual identity
- Selected states

Meaning:
- Self-discovery
- Creativity
- Individuality

---

### Secondary — Soft Rose

Hex: `#E8A0BF`

Use for:
- Cards
- Highlights
- Small accents
- Emotional or warm moments
- Secondary visual elements

Meaning:
- Connection
- Reflection
- Humanity

---

### Accent — Golden Yellow

Hex: `#F6D365`

Use for:
- Important call-to-action moments
- Progress indicators
- Milestones
- Growth moments
- Positive achievements

Meaning:
- Confidence
- Achievement
- Improvement

Use this color intentionally rather than everywhere so that it retains meaning.

---

### Background — Warm Cream

Hex: `#FFF8F0`

Use for:
- Main application background
- Empty space
- Main screens
- Large surface areas

Meaning:
- Calm
- Safety
- Openness

Avoid pure white as the primary application background unless needed inside a component.

---

### Text — Soft Charcoal

Hex: `#333333`

Use for:
- Body text
- Labels
- Icons
- Supporting information

Avoid pure black when possible.

---

## Visual Direction

Use generous whitespace and rounded shapes.

The application should feel soft and approachable without looking childish.

Prefer:
- Rounded cards
- Subtle borders
- Gentle shadows
- Large comfortable spacing
- Clear hierarchy
- Simple icons
- Minimal visual clutter

Avoid:
- Harsh gradients
- Neon colors
- Heavy shadows
- Extremely saturated colors
- Corporate-looking dashboards
- Excessive borders
- Overly playful children's-app styling

---

## Buttons

### Primary Button

Background:
`#5B4B8A`

Text:
White

Use for:
- Continue
- Save
- Start recording
- Primary navigation actions

Buttons should have:
- Rounded corners
- Comfortable padding
- Clear hover state
- Clear disabled state

### Highlight / Growth Action

Golden Yellow `#F6D365` may be used selectively for milestone or growth-related actions.

---

## Cards

Cards should generally use:
- Warm cream or slightly lighter surfaces
- Soft Rose accents when appropriate
- Rounded corners
- Very subtle shadows or borders

Cards should feel lightweight rather than boxed-in.

---

## Typography

Use a modern, readable sans-serif typeface.

Typography should feel:
- Friendly
- Clean
- Calm
- Contemporary

Headings should have personality but remain highly readable.

Body copy should prioritize readability.

Do not use overly decorative fonts.

---

## VoiceMap

The VoiceMap is the central visual identity of Haelo.

Deep Violet should be the dominant VoiceMap color.

Soft Rose and Golden Yellow can represent secondary states, highlights, changes, and growth.

The VoiceMap should feel exploratory rather than diagnostic.

Avoid visual language that resembles:
- Medical charts
- Mental health diagnoses
- Grades
- Rankings

The map should communicate:
"This is how your voice changes across different parts of your life."

---

## Overall Rule

When designing a new page or component, prioritize:

1. Clarity
2. Calm
3. Warmth
4. Self-discovery
5. Simplicity

Do not introduce new brand colors without a strong reason.
